Follow these simple steps to install the Music Player app on your "Android" device:

1. Download the latest .apk file.
2. Once the download is complete, open your Downloads folder.
3. Tap the downloaded APK file.
4. If Android shows a security warning, tap Settings.
5. Enable Allow from this source or Install unknown apps for the app you're using to open the APK (such as Chrome or your file manager).
6. Go back to the APK installation screen.
7. Tap Install.
8. Wait for the installation to finish.
9. Tap Open to launch the app.
10. Allow the necessary audio or music permissions when prompted.
11. This allows the app to access music files stored on your device.

You're all set! Open the app, explore your music library, create playlists, and enjoy listening to your favorite songs.
